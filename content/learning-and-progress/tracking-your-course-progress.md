# Tracking Your Course Progress

Evolve makes it easy to see how far you have come and what is left to complete. This guide explains the different ways you can monitor your learning progress across the platform.

## Progress on the Dashboard

Your **Learner Dashboard** provides an at-a-glance summary of your learning status:

- **In Progress**: Courses you have started but not yet completed. Each card shows a progress bar with your completion percentage.
- **Completed**: Courses you have finished. These move to a separate section so your active list stays focused.
- **Overdue**: Any assigned courses that have passed their due date are flagged in red so you can prioritize them.

## Viewing Progress Within a Course

When you open a course, the left sidebar displays all modules and lessons with status indicators:

- **Green checkmark**: You have completed this lesson.
- **Blue circle**: This lesson is in progress (partially viewed or started).
- **Gray circle**: You have not started this lesson yet.

At the top of the course page, a **progress bar** shows your overall completion percentage. This is calculated based on the number of lessons you have completed out of the total.

## Quiz Scores and Attempts

If a course includes quizzes, your scores are tracked under the **My Results** tab within the course:

- Each quiz attempt shows the **date**, **score**, and **pass/fail status**.
- If your organization allows retakes, you can click **Retake Quiz** to try again. Your highest score is used for completion calculations.
- Open-ended questions may show a "Pending Review" status until an instructor grades your response.

## Learning History

To view a comprehensive record of all your learning activity:

1. Click your **avatar** in the top-right corner and select **Learning History**.
2. This page lists every course you have interacted with, along with start dates, completion dates, and scores.
3. Use the **filter** and **sort** options to narrow results by date range, status, or category.
4. Click **Export** to download your learning history as a CSV file for your personal records.

## Certificates

When you complete a course that awards a certificate:

- The certificate is automatically added to your **Achievements** section on the dashboard.
- You receive an email with a PDF copy of the certificate.
- Click **View Certificate** to see it online, or **Download** to save the PDF.
- Use the **Share** button to post your achievement to LinkedIn or copy a shareable link.

## Staying on Track

Here are a few tips to help you stay consistent:

- **Check your dashboard daily** to see upcoming deadlines and continue where you left off.
- **Enable email reminders** in your notification settings to receive nudges about approaching due dates.
- **Set personal goals** by aiming to complete a certain number of lessons per week.

If you believe your progress is not being tracked correctly, try refreshing the page or clearing your browser cache. If the issue persists, contact your organization's support team for assistance.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Why is my progress not updating after I complete a lesson?**

A: First, try refreshing the page or clearing your browser cache. Progress syncs automatically, but a slow or interrupted network connection can occasionally cause a delay. If the issue persists after refreshing, contact your organization's support team so they can verify your progress on the backend.

**Q: How do I download my course certificate?**

A: Navigate to the **Achievements** section on your Learner Dashboard and locate the certificate for the completed course. Click **Download** to save it as a PDF. You will also receive an email with the certificate attached shortly after completing the course.

**Q: Can I retake a course I have already completed?**

A: Yes, if your organization allows retakes. Open the completed course from your dashboard and click **Retake Course**. Your original completion record and certificate will remain in your Learning History, and the new attempt will be tracked separately.

**Q: Where can I view my full learning history?**

A: Click your **avatar** in the top-right corner and select **Learning History**. This page shows every course you have started or completed, including dates, scores, and statuses. You can filter results by date range or status, and export the full list as a CSV file.
