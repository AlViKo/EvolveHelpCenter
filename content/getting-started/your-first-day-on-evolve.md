# Your First Day on Evolve

Welcome to Evolve! This guide will help you get oriented so you can start learning right away. Whether your organization just rolled out the platform or you are a new hire joining an existing workspace, this overview covers everything you need for a smooth start.

## Logging In

You should have received a welcome email from your organization with a link to your Evolve workspace. Click the link and sign in using one of the following methods:

- **Company SSO**: If your organization uses single sign-on, click the **Sign in with SSO** button and enter your corporate credentials.
- **Email and Password**: If you received a temporary password, enter it on the login page. You will be prompted to set a new password on your first login.

## Setting Up Your Profile

After logging in for the first time, take a moment to complete your profile:

1. Click your **avatar** in the top-right corner and select **My Profile**.
2. Upload a **profile photo** so your teammates and instructors can recognize you.
3. Add your **job title** and **department** to help personalize your learning recommendations.
4. Review your **notification preferences** to control which emails you receive.

## Navigating the Learner Dashboard

Your dashboard is your home base. Here is what you will find:

- **My Courses**: A list of all courses assigned to you or that you have enrolled in. Courses are sorted by due date, with the most urgent at the top.
- **Continue Learning**: A quick-access card for the course you were most recently working on. Click it to pick up right where you left off.
- **Achievements**: Badges and certificates you have earned. Share them with your manager or on LinkedIn directly from this section.
- **Announcements**: Messages from your organization's learning team about new courses, deadlines, or platform updates.

## Starting Your First Course

1. On your dashboard, find a course under **My Courses** and click its title.
2. The course overview page shows the description, estimated duration, and a list of modules.
3. Click **Start Course** to begin with the first lesson.
4. Work through each lesson at your own pace. Your progress is saved automatically, so you can close the browser and return anytime.

## Getting Help

If you run into any issues or have questions:

- Browse this **Help Center** for guides and FAQs.
- Click the **chat icon** in the bottom-right corner of any page to reach your organization's support team.
- Check the **Announcements** section on your dashboard for updates from your learning administrators.

We are glad to have you here. Enjoy your learning journey on Evolve!

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: How do I reset my password?**

A: Go to the login page and click **Forgot Password**. Enter the email address associated with your Evolve account and you will receive a reset link within a few minutes. If you use SSO, your password is managed by your organization's identity provider, so contact your IT department instead.

**Q: What browsers does Evolve support?**

A: Evolve supports the latest two versions of Google Chrome, Mozilla Firefox, Microsoft Edge, and Safari. For the best experience, keep your browser up to date and ensure JavaScript is enabled.

**Q: How do I find the courses assigned to me?**

A: After logging in, navigate to your **Learner Dashboard** and look under the **My Courses** section. All courses assigned to you by your organization will appear there, sorted by due date. You can also use the search bar at the top of the page to find a specific course by name.

**Q: How can I contact support if I need help?**

A: Click the **chat icon** in the bottom-right corner of any page to reach your organization's support team in real time. You can also browse the Help Center for self-service guides, or email your organization's learning administrator directly.
