The main menu is located on the left-hand side and allows you to access all the main sections of the platform and manage courses, users, events, and company settings.

> All menu sections are accessible depending on your role on the platform. Users only see the training and information sections, while administrative functions are available to administrators and account owners.

## Top of the Menu

- **Your name** — the link leads to your dashboard.
- **Administrator / User** — displays the current profile mode.
- **Switch to User / Administrator profile** — allows you to quickly change the mode.

## Control

The main block of administrative tools:

- **Analytics** — reports and statistics on training, user activity, progress, and results.
- **Users** — a list of all company users. Here you can add users, edit data, and manage statuses.
- **Org chart** — build your organizational chart with departments, units, and team members.

## Products

Sections related to courses, events, and professional development of users:

- **Courses** — management of all educational programs: creation, editing, AI-powered generation, assignment, and progress tracking.
- **Events** — online conferences, webinars, and individual sessions.
- **Knowledge Base** — a corporate library where you can search for answers to work questions based on internal documents.
- **Development tracks** — integrated learning paths that combine several courses on a single topic.
- **Scenarios** — automation of actions on the platform: course assignments, reminders, messages.

## Company Account

Settings related to organization management and access rights:

- **Access control** — assigning roles, creating rights, and adding administrators.
- **Billing** — information about the current plan and subscription.
- **Account settings** — general company parameters, logo, description, contact information.

## Settings

- **Download the app** — transition to installation of the mobile application.
- **Profile settings** — editing personal data: name, photo, position, contact information.
- **Language selection** — choice of interface language.
- **Exit** — log out from your account.
