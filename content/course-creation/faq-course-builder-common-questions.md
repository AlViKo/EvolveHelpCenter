# FAQ: Course Builder Common Questions

Find answers to the most frequently asked questions about building and managing courses in Evolve.

## What file formats are supported for video uploads?

Evolve supports **MP4**, **MOV**, and **WebM** video formats. The maximum file size for a single video upload is **2 GB**. For larger files, we recommend compressing the video or splitting it into shorter segments. Videos are automatically transcoded for optimal playback across devices.

## Can I reorder modules and lessons after creating them?

Yes. In the course builder, click and drag the **handle icon** on the left side of any module or lesson to reorder it. Changes to ordering are saved automatically. Learners who have already started the course will see the updated order on their next visit.

## How do I duplicate an existing course?

Navigate to **Courses > Course Library**, find the course you want to duplicate, and click the **three-dot menu** on the course card. Select **Duplicate**. A copy of the course is created in draft status with all modules, lessons, and settings intact. You can then rename it and make any necessary changes before publishing.

## Can multiple people edit the same course?

Evolve supports **collaborative editing**. Multiple Content Managers can work on different modules within the same course simultaneously. However, only one person can edit a specific lesson at a time. If another editor has a lesson open, you will see a lock icon and their name displayed next to that lesson.

## What happens to learner progress if I edit a published course?

Editing a published course does not reset existing learner progress. If you add new lessons, they appear as incomplete for learners who have already started the course. If you remove a lesson that a learner has already completed, their overall progress percentage is recalculated automatically.

## How do I add a quiz to my course?

1. Open the course in the builder and navigate to the module where you want the quiz.
2. Click **+ Add Lesson** and select **Quiz** as the lesson type.
3. Add questions using the supported formats: **multiple choice**, **true/false**, or **open-ended**.
4. Set the **passing score** for the quiz under the lesson settings.
5. Save and preview the quiz to ensure it works as expected.

## Can I set a deadline for course completion?

Yes. When assigning a course to users or teams, you can set an optional **due date**. Learners will see the deadline on their dashboard, and automated reminder emails are sent at 7 days, 3 days, and 1 day before the due date. Overdue courses are highlighted in the admin reports.

## Is there a limit on the number of courses I can create?

There is no hard limit on the number of courses. Your available storage depends on your subscription plan. You can check your current storage usage under **Settings > Billing > Usage**.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Does Evolve offer course templates to help me get started?**

A: Yes. When creating a new course, you can choose from a library of pre-built templates organized by industry and topic. Templates include a suggested module structure, placeholder content, and recommended settings. Select a template during the course creation step and customize it to fit your needs.

**Q: Is there a version history for courses so I can revert changes?**

A: Evolve automatically saves a version snapshot each time you publish updates to a course. You can view the full version history by opening the course in the builder and navigating to **Settings > Version History**. From there, you can compare versions side by side and restore any previous version with a single click.

**Q: How do I permanently delete a course?**

A: Navigate to **Courses > Course Library**, click the **three-dot menu** on the course card, and select **Delete**. You will be prompted to confirm, since deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. Courses with active enrollments must first be archived and all learners un-enrolled before deletion is allowed.
