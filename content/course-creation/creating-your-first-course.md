# Creating Your First Course

This guide walks you through building a course from scratch using the Evolve course builder. By the end, you will have a published course ready for learner enrollment.

## Step 1: Start a New Course

1. Navigate to **Courses > Course Library** in the left sidebar.
2. Click the **+ Create Course** button in the top-right corner.
3. Enter a **Course Title** and an optional **Description**. The description appears on the course card and helps learners understand what they will learn.
4. Select a **Category** to organize the course within your library.
5. Click **Create** to open the course builder.

## Step 2: Add Modules and Lessons

Courses in Evolve are organized into **Modules**, and each module contains one or more **Lessons**.

- Click **+ Add Module** to create a new section. Give it a clear title such as "Introduction" or "Module 1: Fundamentals."
- Within a module, click **+ Add Lesson** to insert content. Each lesson can be one of the following types:
  - **Text**: Rich text content with formatting, images, and embedded media.
  - **Video**: Upload a video file or paste a URL from YouTube or Vimeo.
  - **Document**: Attach a PDF, slide deck, or other downloadable file.
  - **Quiz**: Add assessment questions (multiple choice, true/false, or open-ended).

## Step 3: Configure Course Settings

Before publishing, review the settings on the **Course Settings** tab:

- **Duration**: Estimated time to complete the course. This is displayed to learners.
- **Passing Score**: The minimum quiz score required to earn a completion certificate (if applicable).
- **Enrollment Type**: Choose between **Open** (any user can self-enroll) or **Assigned** (admin assigns specific users or teams).
- **Certificate**: Toggle whether a certificate of completion is issued. You can customize the certificate template under **Settings > Certificates**.

## Step 4: Preview and Publish

1. Click the **Preview** button to experience the course as a learner would see it. Navigate through each module and lesson to verify content, ordering, and media playback.
2. When satisfied, return to the course builder and click **Publish**.
3. Confirm the publication. The course is now live and visible in the course library.

## What Happens After Publishing

- Learners can discover the course in the library if enrollment is set to Open.
- If enrollment is set to Assigned, navigate to **Courses > Enrollments** to assign users or teams.
- You can continue editing a published course at any time. Changes are saved as a draft until you click **Publish Updates**.

> **Tip:** Start with a short course of 3-5 lessons to familiarize yourself with the builder before tackling larger content projects.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Is there a maximum course size or lesson limit?**

A: There is no hard limit on the number of modules or lessons per course. However, total storage for uploaded media (videos, documents, images) is governed by your subscription plan. You can check your current usage under **Settings > Billing > Usage**.

**Q: Can I import content from another LMS?**

A: Evolve supports importing courses in **SCORM 1.2**, **SCORM 2004**, and **xAPI** formats. Navigate to **Courses > Course Library**, click **+ Create Course**, and select **Import** instead of starting from scratch. Upload your package file and Evolve will map the content into modules and lessons automatically.

**Q: Can I edit a course after it has been published?**

A: Yes. Open the published course in the course builder and make your changes. Edits are saved as a draft and do not affect the live version until you click **Publish Updates**. Existing learner progress is preserved when you update a published course.

**Q: How do I duplicate an existing course?**

A: Go to **Courses > Course Library**, find the course you want to copy, and click the **three-dot menu** on the course card. Select **Duplicate** to create a draft copy with all modules, lessons, and settings intact. You can then rename it and modify the content before publishing.
