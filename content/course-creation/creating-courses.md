Evolve offers several ways to create courses: **AI-powered generation** from your existing materials, or **manual creation** using the built-in editor, or **Importing SCORM file** in case you need to move from your existing LMS platform. This guide covers both methods, file requirements, and frequently asked questions.

## Course Creation Using AI

### Step 1 — Make sure you are logged in as an administrator

In the left menu at the very top, your name is displayed. Right under it, you can see your status: "Administrator" or "User".

If you see the status "User", you need to switch to the administrator profile. Click the icon with two arrows or the text **Switch to Administrator Profile**.

After switching, the admin panel will open, where all platform management functions are available: adding users, managing courses, setting up a company profile, and more.

### Step 2 — Navigate to course creation

In the left menu, select **Courses**, then click **Create a course** in the upper right corner.

In the window that opens, select the option **Build with AI** and press **Select**.

### Step 3 — Complete the course form

Enter all the information about the future course in the form:

- The purpose and topic of the course
- Target audience
- Wishes for the format
- Upload documents or materials on which to base the course

There is no need to write a prompt — the system already contains built-in templates. Your task is to clearly describe the course objectives and expectations. AI will do the rest.

### Step 4 — Start generation

After filling out the form, click the **Create a course** button. The course will begin generating automatically — you can close the page or even your browser; the process is independent of your device. Average generation time is up to 24 hours.

> Before submitting, make sure all fields are filled in correctly. If you notice an error after launching, contact the support chat as soon as possible (the blue icon in the lower right corner of the platform) or send an email to support@evolveplatform.ai

### Step 5 — Notification of readiness

When the generation is completed, the account owner and administrator will receive an email notification that the course is ready.

### Step 6 — Support

If you have any questions about the platform or the generation process:

- Open support chat in the lower right corner
- Write your question and the team will answer and help you figure it out
- Or send an email to support@evolveplatform.ai

## AI Generation: Resources and Limitations

Each course is created exclusively using the materials you upload. The platform does not use external sources or search for information online — only your content.

We care about the quality of our courses, so we have introduced restrictions on the size and number of uploaded files:

| Material type | Limits |
|---|---|
| Files per course | Up to 5 |
| Presentations (.pptx) | Max 100 MB, up to 100 slides |
| Text documents (.doc, .docx, .xls, .xlsx) | Up to 20 MB |
| PDF documents | Up to 50 MB, max 80 pages (10pt font) |
| Video content | Total length no more than 2 hours 30 minutes |

**Why is this important?** Too much material can reduce the quality of a course — the information becomes overwhelming, difficult to digest, and less effective for learning.

At maximum volumes, this is approximately 400 pages of text, or 500 slides, or up to 2.5 hours of video and audio materials per course.

## Creating a Course Manually

You can create a new course manually using two options: duplicating an existing course or clicking the **New course** button and selecting **Create manually**.

### Duplicating an existing course

1. Go to the **Courses** section.
2. Choose the desired course and click the three dots in the top right corner.
3. Select **Duplicate**.

A copy of the course will appear with the type "Manual" — it is not charged to your account. This is convenient if you want to experiment with materials: try different formats, change the order of lessons, or test the structure without affecting the main course.

### Using the Evolve Studio editor

When you create courses manually, you have access to the Evolve Studio editor, which allows you to make courses more interactive by fine-tuning text and media files.

Once a block is added to your lessons, you can add as many elements as needed. The control panel is available directly in the editor.

By clicking on the **All blocks** button you can see additional controls for making your course more interactive and attractive for your users.

### Flip-cards

One of the useful features available in courses and especially in tests are **flip-cards** — interactive elements that learners can click to reveal additional information.

## What Happens After Generation Starts?

Once you have submitted your materials and clicked **Create Course**, you can safely close the platform. No constant internet connection or open window is required — the course is created by our platform.

Generation time is usually about 24 hours, sometimes faster, sometimes a little longer, depending on the volume and complexity of the materials. We strive to ensure you receive your course within 24 hours and are working to speed up the process.

## How Do You Know When the Course Is Ready?

Once the course is fully generated, you will receive a notification via email — the email address you used to create the course. All colleagues with the Account Owner or Administrator role will also receive the email.

## What Can Be Edited in a Completed Course?

Once the course is created, you can make any edits directly on the platform:

- Edit the course introduction page
- Change chapter/day titles and descriptions
- Edit texts in lessons
- Change or delete tasks
- Replace or remove images
- Add new lessons, assignments, and blocks

You can edit your course yourself directly on the platform — it is quick and easy.

## How to Delegate Course Edits to the Evolve Team

If you prefer, you can collect the edits you need and pass them on to your account manager, write in the support chat, or send an email to support@evolveplatform.ai. The team will make all the changes for you: create alternative tasks, add the necessary blocks, and correct the texts.

## How to Regenerate Content

If you need to regenerate a block or image, submit the edits to your manager or write to the support chat — the team will make the changes for you.

## FAQ

### Is it possible to upload scanned documents?

Yes, just make sure the text is legible. The platform recognizes text using OCR.

### Is it possible to upload a video via a link (YouTube, Vimeo) instead of a file?

No, you need to upload a video file in .mp4 format.

### Is it possible to upload an archive (.zip, .rar) with multiple files?

No, you need to upload files separately. This allows the platform to process each file correctly.

### What if I need to upload more than five materials?

We recommend dividing the material into several courses. After generating the courses, you can combine them to create a coherent learning process within a single course or learning track.

### What if my file is the wrong size or format?

If the file exceeds the allowed size, try optimizing it (compressing images, removing unnecessary elements) or splitting it into several parts. If the format is not supported, convert the file to one of the supported formats.

If you encounter any difficulties, contact support via the chat icon in the lower right corner of the platform.

### I forgot to upload an item. How do I add it?

If you forgot to upload materials and the generation process has already started, contact the support chat in the lower right corner and request additional materials for the newly launched course. It is best to do this as soon as possible after sending the course for generation.

### Can the platform independently find missing course information online?

Yes, you can specify topics you want to search further in the course creation form.

### Can the platform create a course without input materials?

If you do not have any ready-made materials, contact your account manager. Our team of methodologists will help you select and prepare the necessary content for your course and help you launch the generation process.

### If I duplicate a course created by AI, will this incur additional costs?

No. When you create a copy of a course, it is assigned the "Manual" type — it is not charged to your account.
