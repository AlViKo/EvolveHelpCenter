# Configuring Your Company Profile

Your company profile in Evolve controls how your organization appears to learners and shapes the overall look and feel of the platform. Completing this setup ensures a consistent, branded experience for everyone in your workforce.

## Accessing Company Settings

1. Click the **gear icon** in the left sidebar or navigate to **Settings > Company Profile**.
2. You will see the Company Profile editor with several tabs: **General**, **Branding**, **Localization**, and **Notifications**.

## General Information

Under the **General** tab, fill in the following fields:

- **Company Name**: Your organization's official name as it will appear across the platform.
- **Industry**: Select your industry from the dropdown. This helps Evolve recommend relevant course templates.
- **Company Size**: Choose the range that matches your organization (e.g., 50-200 employees).
- **Primary Contact Email**: The email address used for platform-related communications from Evolve.
- **Support URL**: An optional link to your internal IT support or help desk.

## Branding

The **Branding** tab allows you to customize the visual identity of your Evolve workspace:

- **Logo**: Upload your company logo in PNG or SVG format. Recommended size is 200x60 pixels. This logo appears in the top-left corner of the platform and on certificates.
- **Favicon**: Upload a small icon (32x32 pixels) that appears in browser tabs.
- **Primary Color**: Set the accent color used for buttons, links, and highlights. Enter a hex code or use the color picker.
- **Login Background**: Upload a custom background image for the login page. Supported formats are JPG and PNG, with a recommended resolution of 1920x1080.

## Localization

Under the **Localization** tab, configure regional preferences:

- **Default Language**: Choose the default interface language for new users. Evolve supports over 20 languages.
- **Timezone**: Set the organization-wide default timezone. Individual users can override this in their personal settings.
- **Date Format**: Select your preferred date display format (e.g., DD/MM/YYYY or MM/DD/YYYY).

## Notification Preferences

The **Notifications** tab controls platform-wide email notification defaults:

- **Welcome Email**: Toggle whether new users receive an automated welcome email upon account creation.
- **Course Assignment Alerts**: Enable or disable email notifications when learners are assigned a new course.
- **Completion Certificates**: Choose whether certificates are automatically emailed to learners upon course completion.

## Saving and Previewing

After making changes, click **Save Changes** at the bottom of the page. Use the **Preview** button to see how your branding looks from a learner's perspective before publishing.

> **Tip:** Changes to branding elements like logo and colors take effect immediately for all users after saving. Consider making updates during off-peak hours to minimize confusion.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Can I change the company name after initial setup?**

A: Yes. Go to **Settings > Company Profile > General** and update the **Company Name** field. The new name takes effect immediately across the platform, including on certificates and email notifications. Note that previously issued certificates will retain the name that was active at the time they were generated.

**Q: What are the logo file requirements?**

A: Logos must be in **PNG** or **SVG** format with a recommended size of 200x60 pixels. The maximum file size is 5 MB. For best results, use a transparent background so the logo blends seamlessly with the platform's color scheme.

**Q: Does Evolve support multiple brand profiles for different teams or subsidiaries?**

A: Evolve currently supports one brand profile per workspace. If your organization requires separate branding for different divisions, you can set up additional Evolve workspaces, each with its own company profile and branding configuration. Contact your account manager to discuss multi-workspace options.

**Q: How do I update billing information for my account?**

A: Navigate to **Settings > Billing > Payment Method** to update your credit card, invoicing details, or billing address. Changes to billing information apply to the next billing cycle. For questions about your subscription plan or pricing, contact your Evolve account representative.
